DREAMSCAPE

Personal Project


Description

"Dreamscape" is a round-based survival game that I worked on from August to September 2025. I consider this game to be one of my most polished projects due to the level of organization of its systems and features. After just a few weeks, the game was released and published in September of 2025.

The overall concept of this game is completely original, though it was inspired by other round-based games like Murder Mystery 2 and Super Bomb Survival. I also owe a lot to my close friends, as they contributed some amazing ideas and feedback throughout the development of this project.

Game Page: https://www.roblox.com/games/95653486308203/Dreamscape
